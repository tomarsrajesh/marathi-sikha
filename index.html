🙏 About मराठी शिका

A free app to help anyone learn Marathi & Sanskrit — made with ❤️ by @mainormerimumbai.

✨ How to use

  • Type in English, Hindi or Hinglish
  • Get both Marathi + Sanskrit translation
  • Tap 🎤 to speak
  • Tap 🔊 to hear pronunciation
  • Tap story circles for quick phrases
